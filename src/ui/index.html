About

Clock-synchronised personal linear television for Stremio.

This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. TV metadata may also be provided by TVmaze.

Self-hosted under the MIT License. Keep the editor on localhost, a trusted LAN, or a private VPN; it has no built-in authentication.

Saving writes channels.json atomically, keeps a .bak, hot reloads, and rebuilds only channels whose programming changed.
